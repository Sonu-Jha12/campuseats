# CampusEats — System Brief

## What is CampusEats?

CampusEats is a campus food ordering and delivery system designed for students and campus food vendors. It allows students to browse available food items, place orders, make payments, and track their orders. Vendors can manage their menus and process incoming orders.

## Who Will Use It?

### Students

Students use CampusEats to:

- Browse food items and menus.
- Search for food.
- Add items to a cart.
- Place food orders.
- Make payments.
- Track order status.
- View previous orders.

### Campus Food Vendors

Vendors use CampusEats to:

- Create and manage their food menu.
- Add, update, or remove food items.
- Receive student orders.
- Accept or reject orders.
- Update order status.
- View completed orders.

### Campus Administrator

The administrator manages the overall system and can:

- Manage students and vendors.
- Monitor orders.
- Manage reported issues.
- View system activity.

## Basic System Flow

A student logs into CampusEats and browses food items offered by campus vendors. The student selects food items and adds them to a cart. After reviewing the cart, the student places an order and completes the payment. The vendor receives the order, accepts it, prepares the food, and updates the order status. The student can track the order until it is delivered.

## Summary

CampusEats connects students with campus food vendors through a simple web-based food ordering system. The main purpose is to make campus food ordering faster, easier, and more organized.
